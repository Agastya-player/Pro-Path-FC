Our first website

The story behind PP Pro Path FC - Merulas.

This is our first website — and I created it with my cousins.

We are a family team who love football. We wanted a place to celebrate the players we look up to, from the legends our parents told us about to the stars we watch every week.

PP Pro Path FC - Merulas is where we started learning how websites are made. It's our first one, so we are still learning, and we plan to keep adding more players, more facts and new features.
