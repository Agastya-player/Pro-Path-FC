About us

Pure water. No compromises.

Royal Challenge Water was started with one simple idea: everyone deserves clean, great-tasting drinking water. We source water carefully, purify it with modern technology and pack it in hygienic, sealed containers.

We supply every kind of customer, from retailers and shops to country clubs, bars and restaurants, catering services and big family gatherings. Whether it's a single case or a truckload, we take care of the quality and you just enjoy the water.
